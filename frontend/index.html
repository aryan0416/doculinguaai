How to Use DocuLinguaAI

🚀 Getting Started

  1. Sign in with your Google Account.
  2. Click or drag a file onto the upload area.
  3. Click "Analyze Document" and wait for the magic.
  4. Explore results in the tabs (Text, Summary, etc.).
  5. Use the on-demand translate button in the 'Text' tab.
  6. Use the Q&A section to ask specific questions.

📋 Features

  • Secure Sign-In: Your session is secured with Google Authentication.
  • Multi-format Support: PDF, DOCX, TXT, and common image files.
  • On-Demand Translation: Translate to over 10 major languages when you need it.
  • AI Analysis: Get summaries, keywords, sentiment, and named entities from the original text.
  • Interactive Highlighting: Entities are highlighted directly in the original text.
  • AI Q&A: Get intelligent, context-aware answers.
  • Document History: Access and manage your recently processed documents.

💡 Pro Tips

  • For best results with images (OCR), use clear, high-resolution files.
  • The main analysis is performed on the original text for speed and accuracy.
  • Your document history is stored securely on our servers.
